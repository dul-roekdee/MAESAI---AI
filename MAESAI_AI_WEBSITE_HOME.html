MAESAI AI

From Memories to Music & MV

An AI creative system designed to transform personal images, emotions, stories and music preferences into personalized songs and music videos. MAESAI AI is being developed to make creative production simple for ordinary users.

Prototype / API integration in development
01

Personal Images

Start with personal images and the memories or people they represent.

02

Emotion

Describe how you want the finished work to feel. MAESAI AI turns the feeling into creative direction.

03

Music Preference

Choose music style, instruments, vocal character and mood without needing complex prompts.

04

Story & Lyrics

The system is designed to develop a story and lyrics from the user's intent and emotional context.

05

Music

Connect to an appropriate music-generation service to produce the customized song.

06

MV

Map story, lyrics, images and emotion into scenes for a personalized music video workflow.

MAESAI AI Creative Flow

Image → Emotion → Story → Lyrics → Music → Scenes → MV → Verify

The goal is not simply to make a slideshow. MAESAI AI is designed as an orchestration layer that interprets the user's intent and coordinates the creative workflow.

Current development status

MAESAI AI is currently a prototype. External AI services and production APIs are being evaluated and integrated. This page does not claim that any third-party music, image, voice, video or publishing service is already connected.
